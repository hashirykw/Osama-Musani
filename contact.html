    Questions

    Asked before enrolling.

    Not here? Ask Buzz in the corner, or message Sir Osama.

    Where does Sir Osama teach?

    At EVO Institute in Bahadurabad, which he founded, and at Success Institute in Block L, North Nazimabad. He has also taught Business Studies at Titan College and Axora Academy. Online batches are available for students outside Karachi.

    Which syllabuses does he teach?

    Cambridge O Level Business (7115, becoming 7081 from 2027), Cambridge IGCSE Business (0450, becoming 0264 from 2027) and Cambridge International AS & A Level Business 9609, from AS Papers 1 and 2 to A Level Papers 3 and 4.

    I sit O Level in 2026. Which syllabus is mine?

    2026 candidates take 7115 (or 0450 for IGCSE). From 2027 the new codes take over with the same two papers and a few new topics. He teaches both, so ask which applies to your series.

    What are the fees and timings?

    They depend on your level, the campus and the batch, so Sir Osama quotes them directly. Send the form above and he will reply on WhatsApp.

    Can I see how he teaches first?

    His Instagram, @econobuz, has case files, the Judging the Billboard series and AS Business explainers. Ask about a trial class at your campus.

    What results do his students get?

    Three of his AS students posted 40/40 on 9609 Paper 12 in the June 2026 series; the marks reports are on the home page.

    How does the student portal work?

    Enrolled students receive a student ID and an access code. Signing in shows lectures and revision sessions for your level, announcements and your test marks. You can try the demo now with the sample student.

    Where do I find past papers?

    On the past papers page: 7115, 0450 and 9609 in the current format, with mark schemes, a score tracker and an exam timer.

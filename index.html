How Paper 1 is marked

Short answers, then one essay.

Questions 1 to 4 are short answers worth 20 marks. Then you choose question 5 or 6: a two-part essay worth 20, part (a) for 8 and part (b) for 12, with the marks split by skill.

  • AO1Knowledge
  • AO2Application
  • AO3Analysis
  • AO4Evaluation

That AO split is exactly what the reports show for 5a and 5b. Full marks means every skill was hit, not just the facts.

The method

From a billboard to a full-mark answer.

Six moves, in the order the mark scheme rewards them. Every unit runs through the same six, so by the exam they are habit.

  1. 01Campaign

    Start with the real thing

    A launch, an ad, a crisis or a billboard from this week. Students meet the idea in the wild before they meet the definition.

    • Case files from @econobuz
    • The Judging the Billboard series
    • Local brands students already buy
  2. 02AO1

    Name the concept

    Then the textbook term, defined the way the mark scheme words it, so the knowledge marks are banked.

    • Key terms in one line each
    • Formulas with a worked number
    • Flashcards in the Lab
  3. 03AO2

    Apply it to the case

    Every point uses the business in the question: its product, its numbers, its customers. General answers stop at the knowledge mark.

    • Pull evidence from the insert
    • Use the figures, not just quote them
    • Name the business in every paragraph
  4. 04AO3

    Build the chain

    Because this, then that, which means this for the business. Analysis is a chain of reasoning that ends at profit, sales or survival.

    • Two developed points beat four listed ones
    • Connectives that show cause and effect
    • Stop when the chain reaches the objective
  5. 05AO4

    Make the call

    A judgement that answers the question, says what it depends on, and would change if the facts changed. The marks most students leave on the table.

    • Recommend, then justify
    • “It depends on” with something specific
    • Short term against long term
  6. 06Exam

    Do it against the clock

    Past papers under timed conditions, marked against the scheme, until the 90 minutes feel normal.

    • Exam timer with focus mode
    • Score tracker with a trend line
    • Mark schemes for every series

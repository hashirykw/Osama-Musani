How he teaches

Two words. One paper. Zero shortcuts.

His own line on AS Business: it is not about writing more, it is about explaining clearly and analysing smartly. In practice that looks like this.

01

Campaigns first

Every unit opens with something real: a launch, a crisis, a billboard. Students meet the idea in the wild before they meet the definition, so the definition has somewhere to stick.

02

A marketer’s eye

An IBA Marketing degree shows up in class: segmentation, positioning and promotion are taught the way agencies use them, then tied back to the mark scheme.

03

Explain, then analyse

Two developed points beat four listed ones. Every answer is a chain that ends at sales, costs, profit or survival, applied to the business in the question.

04

A room people want to be in

Table tennis tournaments, a gaming lounge at EVO, BBQ nights, reels his students star in. Students who enjoy the room turn up, and students who turn up do the work.

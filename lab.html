Tool 01 · 7081 4.4 · 9609 5.4

Break-even with what-ifs

Enter the costs and the price. The chart, the working and the margin of safety update as you type. Then try a what-if: exam questions love asking what happens when one figure changes.

Tool 02 · 7081 5.5 · 9609 10.2

Ratio analysis

Enter figures from an income statement and a statement of financial position. You get each ratio, the formula with your numbers in it, and what it says about the business.

Tool 03 · 7081 5.2 · 9609 5.3

Cash-flow forecast

Edit any figure. Net cash flow and the opening and closing balances recalculate, and the chart shows exactly which month would need an overdraft.

Tool 04 · 9609 10.3

Investment appraisal

Enter the cost of the investment and its net cash flow each year. Payback, average rate of return and net present value are worked out in full, with discount factors to three decimal places, the way exam tables give them.

Tool 05 · 9609 8.1 · 7081 3.3

Price elasticity

Change the price and the quantity sold. See the PED, whether demand is elastic, and what the price change did to revenue: the reason a business cares.

Tool 06 · 7081 3.1–3.4 · 9609 3.3, 8.2

Billboard judge

His Judging the Billboard series, as a tool. Pick the next billboard you pass, score it on the questions an examiner would ask, and get a structured paragraph to finish in your own words.

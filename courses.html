O Level Business

Six units, from what a business is to how the economy and the wider world change its decisions. Two compulsory 90-minute papers, graded A* to E in the June and November series.

AS & A Level Business

Ten units over two years. AS covers the core of every department in Papers 1 and 2. The A Level year adds strategy, finance and decision-making in Papers 3 and 4, which assume everything from AS.

7115 → 7081 · 0450 → 0264

What changes in 2027.

Students sitting in 2026 still take 7115 or 0450. From 2027 the new codes take over. Sir Osama teaches both, so nobody gets caught between them.

Ask which applies to you
01New topics

Technology in production (4.2) and sustainable production (4.3).

02Digital communication

Added to People in business, with a formulas and ratios list you are expected to use.

03More marks for application

AO2 rises to 30%. Paper 1 questions have four parts, and every Paper 2 part (a) is applied to the case.
